Wait, what's this all about?

A night to make, learn, and belong.

Campus Maker Night is a hands-on event where students from all backgrounds come together to build, create, and explore. Whether you're into robotics, art, 3D printing, coding, or just curious, there's something for you! Tell your friends. Bring a project. Or don't. Just come hang out.

Plan the night (or don't)

What's happening & when?

Times and room locations may change. Follow the signs when you arrive. Browse around to learn more.

5:00 PMDoors open!

Music, mingling, supplies, welcome table, and check-in.

6:00 PMMini workshops

3D printing, coding, circuits, and more in different rooms.

7:30 PMShow & tell

Students share what they've been making or learning.

9:30 PMLate-night making

Open-ended projects, snacks, community, and demos.

🤝 Help Out

Love events? Help move tables, welcome people, and keep the creative energy going. Ask about volunteering at the information table.

💡 Bring Your Idea

Don't have a project? No problem. But if you've got something you'd like to show, we'd love to see it!

🍕 Snacks & Perks

We'll have snacks, drinks, and some fun surprises. Food is limited, so be sure to explore the rest of the event too.

Before you get here...

Some workshops are located in the main hall while others are in separate campus rooms. Keep an eye out for colorful signs, ask a volunteer, and bring your campus ID if you have one.

You'll also want to think about which activity sounds fun, whether you need borrowed equipment, and if you plan on bringing anything along.

You asked, we answered (maybe)

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

It's free! Some workshops have limited capacity, so arriving early is smart.

Can I bring a friend?

Sure, subject to campus guest policies. Everyone should check in at the welcome table.

Do I need experience?

No experience needed. Start with what you're curious about.

Can I switch workshops?

Usually, depending on space. Check with workshop hosts when you arrive.
